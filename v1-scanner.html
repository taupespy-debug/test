Emberline Peak Scanner

This only reads from your Peak. It doesn't change any settings.

Steps
  1. Close the Puffco app fully (swipe it away). Only one app can connect at a time.
  2. Open this page in Path or Bluefy on iPhone, or Chrome on Android or a computer.
  3. Turn your Peak on and tap 1. Connect. Pick your Peak from the list.
  4. Wait for Scan done.
  5. Tap 2. Record 60s, then right away start a heat cycle on the Peak (double click the button). Let it heat. Take a hit if it gets to Ready.
  6. Tap Copy results and send it to kona991 on Discord.
