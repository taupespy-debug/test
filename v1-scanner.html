Hit test

  1. Start a session on the Proxy (double click) and wait until it's Ready.
  2. Tap Record 90s.
  3. Take 3 or 4 hits of different lengths (short, medium, long). Press and hold the big HOLD WHILE HITTING button the whole time you're pulling, let go when you stop.
  4. Also leave it alone for 15 seconds without hitting, so we can see the fake hits.
  5. Let it finish, then tap Copy log and send it to kona991.
