Emberline Alerts

1v1 invites and new followers on your lock screen

Add this to your Home Screen first

iPhone only lets a page send notifications once it's on your Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or newer). Do it in Safari:

  1. Tap the Share ⬆︎ button at the bottom.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Ember Alerts from your Home Screen and come back to this screen.

Not in Safari? Copy this page's link and open it in Safari. Path can't show notifications itself.

This phone can't do notifications here

You need iOS 16.4 or newer, and this page opened from your Home Screen (or Chrome on Android).

Connect your Emberline profile
  1. In Emberline (in Path), open Settings > Phone alerts and tap Get a code.
  2. Type the 6 letters here and tap Turn on alerts. Allow notifications when asked.

Alerts are on

You'll get a notification when a friend invites you to a 1v1 or someone follows you. Tap it, then open Emberline in Path to play.

Open Emberline to play

Open Path and go to Emberline. Invites wait there for 15 minutes under the 🔔 in Social.
