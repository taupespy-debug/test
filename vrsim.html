Emberline VR simulator

The real VR panel, floating in a fake headset view. Everything in the window works like it would in a headset: link your phone with the code, or connect a Peak straight to this computer.

Open panel alone
Simulated headset · test build
Link your phone

The panel shows a 6-character code. On your phone open Emberline (the test link for Test build), go to Settings → VR panel and type it. The live build only links for Rich.

Click the Puffco

Once the panel is linked (or connected), point at the Puffco on the table and click it to start your real one. Click again while it heats or is Ready to boost.

Or connect here

On a computer with Chrome or Edge, the panel can connect a Peak straight over Bluetooth. Safari and Path can’t do Bluetooth inside this simulator, so use the phone link there.

In a real headset

Open emberlineapp.github.io/emberline/?vr in the Quest browser, or in a desktop overlay like XSOverlay, OVR Toolkit or Desktop+.
